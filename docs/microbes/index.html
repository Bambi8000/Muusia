Try it in Muusia

Open Help → Microbes and load an example, or download a patch and use Load. Choose 24 or 48 ANIMATE frames and press Play. Timeline needs no additional wires.

For one frame per sheet, connect Microbes to Frame Grid with a 1 × 1 layout, Light outlines and frame numbers. To plot colours in separate batches, choose All frames by pen. Keep Seed and Count fixed throughout the loop.
